AI Advice Calibration Task

Prototype: live LLM wording + controlled numerical advice + neural spoken delivery

The AI model controls wording only. The experiment calculates the numerical advice and whether that advice is helpful or misleading before the model is called.




This prototype uses a neural AI-generated voice. Use the selector only to choose a voice during development; in a formal study, lock one voice and use it for every assistant, condition, and participant.
