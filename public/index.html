What happens on each trial?

You estimate the number of dots. The AI gives a numerical estimate. You may revise your answer. You then answer one short 1–7 question before seeing the correct number.

The two fixed questions are balanced across trials. With 60 trials, the numerical advice schedule contains 45 helpful and 15 misleading trials in randomized four-trial blocks.
