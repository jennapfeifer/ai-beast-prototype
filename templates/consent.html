BEFORE YOU BEGIN

About this study

You will make an initial and a final estimate for each dot image. {% if ratings %}After every {{rating_every}} trials in a round, you will answer two short questions about trust and how the advice made you feel.{% endif %}

You can rest between rounds. There is no reward for speed. The field map marks completion and does not show accuracy or agreement with the assistant. The correct counts will not be revealed.

The full study’s duration is being measured in pilots. Short advice notes and background preparation reduce reading and waiting; breaks are available between rounds. An estimation summary appears only after you finish.

The assistant’s recommendations are generated for this study and may be inaccurate. Some design details are withheld until the debrief so they do not influence responses.

Your responses are saved under a random participant code. You may stop at any time by closing this tab. Your choices, adviser messages and task timings are recorded; a debrief explains the study and how to request withdrawal.
