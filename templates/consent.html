Dot estimation with an AI assistant

This study looks at how people estimate quantities and how they use written advice from an AI assistant. It takes about 40–45 minutes.

What you'll do

  • You'll see images containing many dots, one at a time.
  • For each image you give a first estimate of how many dots there are.
  • An AI assistant then gives you a written estimate.
  • You give a final estimate. After every two trials, you answer two short questions about trust and how the advice made you feel.
  • There are 104 images in total, split into 8 rounds, with a checkpoint and optional break between rounds.
  • You will never be told the correct number of dots. There is no score and no feedback on accuracy.

Things to know before you agree

  • The AI assistant's estimates are generated for the purposes of this study and are not guaranteed to be accurate. Please treat them as one input, not as a correct answer.
  • Some details of the study design are withheld until the end so they don't influence your responses. You'll get a full explanation on the final page, and you can withdraw your data then.
  • Your responses are stored under a random code, not your name.
  • You can stop at any point by closing the tab, without giving a reason.
