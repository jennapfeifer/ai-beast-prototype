About this study

During the task you will make numerical judgments about visual or social information and receive recommendations from different advisers. On selected trials, you will also briefly describe what influenced your first judgment. {% if ratings %}At intervals, you rate how much you trusted the current adviser.{% endif %}

You can take breaks between rounds. There is no reward for speed or for agreeing with the advice.

The advice may be inaccurate. Some study details are explained only after you finish.

Your judgments, short explanations, advice, ratings and timings are saved under a random participant code. You may stop at any time by closing this tab.
