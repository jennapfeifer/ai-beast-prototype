Dot estimation with an AI assistant

This study looks at how people estimate quantities and use AI advice. It takes about 35–45 minutes.

What you'll do

  • Estimate the number of dots in 104 images, split into 8 rounds.
  • After each first estimate, an AI assistant gives an estimate and a short comment.
  • Choose a final estimate on a number line. Every two trials, answer two quick check-in questions.
  • No correctness feedback is shown during the task. An overall estimation summary appears at the end.

Before you agree

  • Your responses are stored under a random code, not your name.
  • You can stop at any point by closing the tab.
