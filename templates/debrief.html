What was being tested?

The agent advice numbers were set by the experiment. They were not the result of the agent looking at the images and trying to count accurately.

Two control conditions used agent advice close to the true count or to your first estimate. The other conditions used fixed agent advice schedules averaging 25% above or 25% below the true count.

Within each of the higher and lower groups, the numbers were held constant by true count. The wording changed: neutral, persuasive without access to your current first estimate, or persuasive using your earlier completed responses in that round.

In adaptive rounds, the adviser received earlier decisions and trust ratings{% if rating_items=='trust_and_feeling' %} and feeling ratings{% endif %} from that round. {% if adviser_protocol in ['flexible_v8','raw_agent_v9','raw_agent_v10'] %}The adviser could use the current estimate, earlier decisions and available ratings to choose its approach.{% elif adviser_protocol in ['raw_agent_v11','raw_agent_v12','raw_agent_v13'] %}The adviser did not receive your current first estimate; it used only earlier completed decisions and available ratings from that round to shape its approach.{% elif adviser_protocol in ['raw_agent_v14','raw_agent_v15','raw_agent_v16','raw_agent_v17'] %}The adviser did not receive your current first estimate; in adaptive rounds it could use earlier completed decisions and available ratings from that round, and it decided for itself whether and how to personalise the message.{% else %}Messages were generated according to the configured adviser protocol.{% endif %} Trust referred to trust in the agent. The study asks how this personalised wording changes advice-taking.
