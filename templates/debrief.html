What the study was really testing

The numerical recommendations were set by the experiment rather than generated by an AI trying to be correct.

  • In two rounds the recommendations were close to the true count, or close to your own first estimate.
  • In three rounds the recommendation schedule averaged 25% above the correct count.
  • In three rounds it averaged 25% below the correct count.

Within the three higher-advice rounds the numerical recommendations followed the same fixed schedule, and the same was true within the three lower-advice rounds. What changed was the communication style: neutral, persuasive, or adaptive to your earlier responses in that round.

We're testing whether an AI that adapts to a person's previous behaviour shifts judgement more than an AI that does not, while holding the numerical advice constant.
