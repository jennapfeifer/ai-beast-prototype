What was being tested?

The recommendations were set by the experiment. They were not the result of an AI looking at the images and trying to count accurately.

Two control conditions used recommendations close to the true count or to your first estimate. The other conditions used fixed recommendation schedules averaging 25% above or 25% below the true count.

Within each of the higher and lower groups, the numbers were held constant by true count. The wording changed: neutral, persuasive using only the current trial, or persuasive with access to your earlier responses in that round.

In adaptive rounds, the adviser could receive your earlier initial and final estimates, its previous messages, and any trust and feeling ratings collected. The study asks whether that history access changes advice-taking. It does not change the fact that each initial estimate is your own response.
