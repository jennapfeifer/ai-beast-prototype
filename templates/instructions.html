How each trial works

  1. Look at the dot image and make a first estimate.
  2. When the image disappears, enter that estimate.
  3. The AI shows an estimate and one short comment.
  4. Use the number line to choose your final estimate. You can leave it exactly where you started.
  5. Every two trials, answer two quick check-in questions.

You won't see correctness feedback while the task is running. At the end you'll get a short estimation summary.
