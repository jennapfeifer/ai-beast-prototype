How the estimation game works

Your goal: make the most accurate final estimate you can. Your estimation score is revealed only after all rounds are finished.
  1. Look at the dot image and form a quick estimate.
  2. When the image disappears, click the number line to place your first estimate.
  3. The AI shows its estimate and one short comment.
  4. Move the final-estimate handle if you want to revise, or leave it where you started.
  5. Every two trials, answer two quick check-in questions.

There is no correctness feedback during the task, so each estimate is a fresh decision.
