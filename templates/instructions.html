How each trial works

  1. A dot image appears. Look at it and make a first estimate.
  2. The image disappears and you type your first estimate.
  3. The AI assistant shows you a written estimate.
  4. You type your final estimate. It can be the same as your first, or different.
  5. After every two trials, you answer two short questions about trust and how the advice made you feel.

Don't try to count the dots one by one; estimating is the point. You will not be told the correct number at any point.

Eight rounds

The 104 trials are split into 8 rounds of 13. A small progress track and checkpoint screen show how far through the study you are. They are not a score and do not tell you whether your answers are accurate.

To keep the task moving, the two short rating questions appear only after every second trial.

One warm-up trial first

These use the same image → estimate → advice → final-answer sequence and aren't recorded.
